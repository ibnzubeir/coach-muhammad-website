MY STORY

The journey behind Coach Muhammad.

A personal journey of learning, wellness, consistency and helping others create better everyday habits.

MY JOURNEY

More than a wellness journey.

My journey into wellness wasn't about discovering one perfect solution. It was about learning, trying new approaches and understanding how everyday choices can shape the way we live.

Along the way, I became passionate about sharing what I learned with other people and encouraging them to take positive steps toward their own wellbeing.

That's what led me to become Coach Muhammad.

MY EXPERIENCE

A moment that changed my perspective.

There was a time when my own wellbeing became a major part of my life.

That experience taught me the importance of paying attention to my lifestyle, learning more about wellness and being consistent with positive daily habits.

I explored different approaches and eventually came across the FeelGreat program from Unicity.

I decided to include FeelGreat as part of my personal wellness journey, alongside learning more about nutrition, lifestyle and healthy habits.

PART OF MY JOURNEY

Discovering FeelGreat.

FeelGreat became one of the things I explored during my wellness journey. It made me even more interested in daily routines, consistency and taking a more intentional approach to wellbeing.

Today, I share information about the program with people in my community who want to learn more about it.
